# Product vision

SeaLink helps European seafood companies discover industrial partners and move from discovery to structured commercial sourcing. Organizations can carry several business activities simultaneously.

The preview connects professional profiles, supplier and buyer discovery, RFQs, quotations and processing capacity. It is intended for founder-led demonstrations and industry feedback before production infrastructure.

Success means a participant can find a relevant processor, save it, define an RFQ, select suppliers using transparent criteria, simulate responses and understand commercial differences. Trust depends on clear separation between self-reported claims, unknown requirements and verified evidence. No evidence is independently verified in this milestone.

Future production work requires tenant isolation, secure accounts, audited permissions and explicit consent for external delivery. Those systems are intentionally absent here.
